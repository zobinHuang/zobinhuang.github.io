About Me

Zhuobin Huang (Zobin) is a PhD student from Xtra Compute Group, National University of Singapore since 2025, supervised by Prof. Bingsheng He. His current research focuses on Enhencing observability of large-scale GPU systems. He obtained his M.Eng and B.Eng from University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2024 and 2021 respectively, advised by Prof. Shizhong Xu. He served as research assistant in IPADS, Shanghai Jiaotong University from Feb.2023 ~ Apr. 2024, advised by Prof. Xingda Wei. He also worked in industries including Byte Dance, OneFlow and Shanghai AI Lab as R&D internship from 2021~2023.
